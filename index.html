Our Story

Meet the Owner
& The Team

Grandville Barbershop has been a cornerstone of this community since 1961, over 65 years of sharp fades, classic cuts, and old-school craftsmanship on Chicago Drive. The owner and her team carry on that tradition every single day, delivering the kind of consistent, quality work that keeps customers coming back for years.

Step inside and you'll feel it immediately: warm atmosphere, no pretense, just skilled barbers who listen and get it right. We cut all hair types, love kids, and walk-ins are always welcome. No appointment? No problem.
